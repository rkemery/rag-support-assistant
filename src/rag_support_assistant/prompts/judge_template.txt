How to decide:
- A claim is supported when the reference answer or a current source article states it, or it follows directly from them. Rewording, writing "$$5.00" as "$$5", and leaving details out are fine.
- A claim is unsupported when it adds a number, fee, limit, time, plan detail, product, step or rule that the reference and the current sources do not state, or states one differently. One unsupported claim is enough to fail "grounded".
- Check every number against the plan it is about. A value that belongs to another plan is wrong.
- An article marked as superseded shows an old version of a policy. It does not support a claim, unless the question asks what changed.
- Courtesy text such as "message us in the app if you need more help" is not a factual claim.
